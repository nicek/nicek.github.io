## English (default)
**Title:** Arcade Hall '93

**Short description:** Run a 90s arcade hall: buy cabinets, play minigames, serve your guests.

**Full description:**
It is 1989. You have just rented a tiny arcade. Fill it with machines, keep the guests happy and turn a dusty room into
the coolest hall in town.

- BUY AND PLACE CABINETS: shooters, paddle games, night drives, pinball and more, each one a real minigame you can play yourself.
- WATCH THE PIXELS LIVE: an isometric hall full of guests who walk in, queue, play and spend their tokens.
- SET RECORDS: a high score on a machine becomes the hall's record and draws a bigger crowd.
- OPEN A SNACK BAR: hire a bartender and sell cola and candy bars.
- EXPAND UPSTAIRS: an abandoned floor with cobwebs and old crates waits for you. Rent it, clean it, renovate it, and open a second hall.
- READ THE DAILY TOKEN: the newspaper tells you what the town wants today.
- COLLECT THE ALBUM: every model has a flyer in the style of the era.
- PLAY IN SHORT SESSIONS: two minutes at a time, one thumb, portrait. Your arcade keeps earning while you are away.

Free to play with optional ads. Ads only make things faster (double your takings, a VIP guest, quicker builders); you
can finish everything without watching a single one. No accounts, no internet needed to play.

Designed for adults who remember the golden age of arcades.

Mamy rok 1989. Właśnie wynająłeś maleńki salon gier. Wypełnij go automatami, dbaj o gości i zamień zakurzone
pomieszczenie w najfajniejszy lokal w mieście.

- KUPUJ I USTAWIAJ AUTOMATY: strzelanki, gry z paletką, nocne wyścigi, flippery i więcej. Każdy automat to prawdziwa minigra, w którą zagrasz sam.

- OTWÓRZ BAR Z PRZEKĄSKAMI: zatrudnij barmana i sprzedawaj colę oraz batony.
- ROZBUDUJ SIĘ NA PIĘTRZE: czeka tam opuszczone piętro z pajęczynami i starymi skrzyniami. Wynajmij je, posprzątaj, wyremontuj i otwórz drugą salę.
