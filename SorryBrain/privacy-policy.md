# Privacy Policy: Sorry, Brain

**Last updated:** 18/10/2026
**Developer:** Nicek_
**Contact:** yt.nicek@gmail.com

Sorry, Brain ("the app") is a puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what data the app
handles and who handles it.

## What stays on your device

The app saves your progress (scenes cleared, scores, experience points) and your settings (language,
theme, sound, accessibility options) on your device. This data is not sent to us: the app has no
account, no sign-in and no server of ours. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show, measure and secure ads, Google may collect and use
data from your device, such as your Android advertising ID, IP address, approximate location,
device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data and do not
add anything to it.

- Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app
  asks for your consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before any ad is requested. You can
  review or change your choice at any time in **Settings, Privacy settings**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).
- How Google handles this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and
  https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

The app asks ad networks to return only content suitable for teenagers and older.

## Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details.
The app receives a confirmation from Google Play that the purchase exists, so it can switch ads off.

## Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where your country sets a higher age for
consent). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your rights

We do not hold personal data about you, so there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or
delete. If you believe we do, or you have a question about this policy, write to the contact address
above. Where applicable you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
