# Privacy Policy – Spin Cycle: Sock Sort

_Effective date: September 28, 2026_

Spin Cycle: Sock Sort ("the game") is an offline puzzle game. This policy explains what data the
game handles and why.

## Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: unlocked levels, level ratings, coins, boosters,
decorations, settings (sound, vibration), your Daily Load results and streak, and unfinished games.
The game has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it does not send this data anywhere.

If you use Android's backup or device-transfer features, Android may include this progress in your
personal backup (for example in your Google account). This is controlled by your device settings.

You can delete this data at any time with **Settings → Reset progress** or by uninstalling the game.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). This is the only part of the game that
uses the internet. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process data
such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact
with ads. Google's handling of this data is described in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your
consent with Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are requested. You can change your choice
later in **Settings → Ad privacy choices**. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your
Android settings.

Rewarded videos (for example Second Chance or double coins) are always optional.

## Sharing

When you tap **Share**, the game creates a text and an image of your result on your device and hands
them to the app you choose. Nothing is shared unless you do this.

## Permissions

- **Internet and network state:** used only by the ads SDK.
- **Vibration:** for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

## Children

The game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
